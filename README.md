# As-SVS
As-Suwayda Online School
Online School Platform – Program Overview

Our online school platform is inspired by the Japanese education model and adapted for remote learning. The curriculum is structured into three levels: Elementary, Junior High, and High School, covering the essential academic subjects that can be delivered effectively online.

1. Elementary School (Grades 1–6, Ages 6–12)

Subjects:

Arabic Language

Mathematics

Science (basic level)

Social Studies

Art

Music

Moral Education

Special Activities (online class events and collaborative projects)


2. Junior High School (Grades 7–9, Ages 12–15)

Subjects:

Arabic Language

Mathematics

Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Earth Science – theoretical introduction)

Social Studies (History, Geography, Civics)

English Language

Art & Music (theory and creative projects)

Technology and Home Economics (adapted for online learning)

Moral Education

Special Activities (virtual clubs and class discussions)


3. High School (Grades 10–12, Ages 15–18)

Subjects:

Arabic Language

Mathematics

Science (advanced levels of Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Earth Science – theoretical focus)

Social Studies (World History, Geography, Civics)

Foreign Language (mainly English, with options for other languages)

Information Technology / Computer Science

Business and Vocational Studies (adapted for online learning)

Art, Music, and Design (theory and digital projects)

Moral Education

Special Activities (student-led online clubs, debates, and cultural exchange)


Key Features of the Platform

Fully Online Delivery – all courses are adapted for remote learning, removing the need for physical attendance.

Interactive Learning

Values & Character Building – moral education and collaborative projects to support social and emotional growth.

Flexibility – suitable for students in areas affected by hardship, ensuring access to quality education anytime, anywhere.
